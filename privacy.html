Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This policy explains how the private Marath Kudumbam family website handles information.

Information we keep

  • Family information added by family members or admins, such as names, relationships, birth years, places, events, notices and photos.
  • Admin account details: name, email address, phone number and sign-in information. Passwords are handled by our login provider and are not stored in readable form.
  • Limited security information, such as records of failed family login attempts.

How we use it

We use this information only to run the family website, control who can access it, review submitted content, and help admins recover their accounts.

Google Drive

The website connects to a Google account controlled by the family so it can store family photos in Google Drive. It requests only the drive.file permission, which lets it create and manage files used with this website. It cannot browse other files in that Drive.

The website's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Who can see it

Family content is available only to people with family access and approved admins. Submitted items stay hidden from the family area until an admin approves them. We do not sell this information or use it for advertising.

We use Supabase for logins, the database and small photo previews, and Google Drive for full-size photos. These providers process information so the website can operate.

Keeping and removing information

Information stays until an admin removes it or it is no longer needed. To ask for something about you or your child to be corrected or removed, email us.

Browser storage

The website may keep a login record in your browser so that you stay signed in. Logging out removes it from that browser.
